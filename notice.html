Laptop Notices & Return Policy

📢 Customer: Laptop Return Policy

Use this notice on product pages, invoices or store noticeboards.
Template

Please read the terms below before requesting a return or replacement.

  1. Return window: Laptops may be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original condition.
  2. Manufacturing defects: Defects reported within the warranty period will be handled per the manufacturer's warranty and our support policy.
  3. Exclusions: Returns are not accepted for damage caused by misuse, accidental drops, liquid damage, or unauthorized repair attempts.
  4. Condition: All accessories, original packaging, and proof of purchase (invoice) must be provided for a valid return.
  5. Refund process: Refund or exchange processing takes up to 7–10 business days after inspection.

🖥️ Company-issued Laptop Return Notice

For HR / IT to post before employee exit or handover.
Notice

All employees who are resigning, finishing their internship, or undergoing a role change that requires device handover must follow the steps below.

  1. Return deadline: Submit the company-issued laptop, charger and bag to the IT department on or before the last working day.
  2. Data backup: Back up any personal files. IT will remove company data and reimage the device after clearance.
  3. Condition check: Devices will be inspected for physical damage. Costs for repairs due to negligence may be charged per company policy.
  4. Clearance: A Clearance Certificate will be issued only after successful return and verification of the device and accessories.
  5. Contact: IT Helpdesk — laptopspace@hotmail.com/em> | Ext: 1234.
